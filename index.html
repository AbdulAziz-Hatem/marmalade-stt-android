Works where you type

Marmalade STT plugs into Android's own voice input, so you keep the keyboard and the apps you already use.

Your keyboard's mic keyTurn on Marmalade voice typing, and the mic key opens a small Marmalade panel that types what you say.
The speak-now popupApps with a "speak now" button get a small Marmalade sheet that returns your words when you finish.
Android's speech recognizerPick Marmalade STT as the speech recognition service, and apps that use it hear you through Marmalade.

What you get

Everything happens on your phone. The only download is the speech engine you choose.

  • Private

    Speech is recognized on your phone, never by a cloud or online speech service. Audio is never saved or uploaded.

  • Offline

    After a one-time download of a speech engine, it works without a connection.

  • Live text

    Your words appear in real time as you speak, on most engines.

  • Knows when you're done

    Smart Turn, the default for the speak-now popup, waits until you sound finished, so a pause to think rarely cuts you off. Or stop at a pause, or say an end word.

  • 100 languages

    The setup guide picks the best engine for your language, and for your phone's memory and speed.

  • Clean text

    Optional cleanup removes filler words, writes numbers as digits and turns "new line" into the real thing, in English. Your own replacements and snippets work in any language.

  • Light, dark, high contrast

    Light or dark, four colors or your wallpaper's (Android 12 and later), and high-contrast colors for easier reading.

  • Free and open source

    MIT licensed. No ads, no accounts, no analytics.

Built on open speech models

Marmalade runs open models directly on your phone. You download only the engine you choose, once, from GitHub, and every file is checked against a pinned SHA-256 hash.

  • ParakeetNVIDIA · Moondream · CC BY 4.0

    The default. Parakeet Ultra, Moondream's post-trained Parakeet TDT 0.6B v3, covers 25 European languages and detects which one you speak. Parakeet 110M is a small, fast English model, and Parakeet Japanese handles Japanese.

    Parakeet Ultra110MJapanese
  • Canary 180M FlashNVIDIA · CC BY 4.0

    A light engine for English, Spanish, German and French, for phones short of memory or speed.

    Hugging Face
  • Nemotron 3.5NVIDIA · OpenMDW-1.1

    Streams text as you speak in Hindi, Arabic and Vietnamese.

    Hugging Face
  • Qwen3-ASRAlibaba Cloud · Apache-2.0

    Chinese and Korean, and lighter to run than Whisper.

    GitHubHugging Face
  • Whisper Large v3 TurboOpenAI · MIT

    Every other language, 100 in all, detected automatically. It is slower on most phones, so it is recommended only where nothing lighter covers your language.

    GitHubHugging Face
  • Smart Turn and Silero VADDaily · BSD-2-Clause · Silero Team · MIT

    Built into the app. Silero VAD hears where speech starts and stops, and Smart Turn v3.2 judges whether you sounded finished.

    Smart TurnSilero VAD

The engines run on sherpa-onnx (Apache-2.0, built from source) and ONNX Runtime (MIT). Full credits and license texts are in the app and in NOTICE.md.

Private by design

No analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting, no accounts, and no server of ours. Speech is recognized on the phone, and audio is never saved or uploaded. The internet is used only to download the speech engines you choose, from GitHub, and every file is checked against a pinned SHA-256 hash. Android backup is turned off, so nothing is copied to the cloud.

Privacy policy

Open source

Marmalade STT is MIT licensed, and the app you install contains no GPL or LGPL code. Its speech runtime, sherpa-onnx, is built from source with the text-to-speech parts left out. Every component's license is listed in the app under Settings › About › Open-source licenses.
