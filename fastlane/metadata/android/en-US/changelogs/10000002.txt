Marmalade STT 1.0.0, the first release.
- Private, offline speech-to-text: a voice keyboard for FUTO, HeliBoard, FlorisBoard and others, a speak-now sheet for apps, and Android's speech recognition service.
- Engines picked for your phone and language, with dedicated picks for Japanese, Chinese, Korean, Hindi, Arabic and Vietnamese.
- Stop by tap, pause, end word or Smart Turn.
- Text cleanup, replacements and snippets.
- The app itself is in 21 languages. No ads, no accounts, no analytics.
