Marmalade STT 1.2.0 is faster and has a new look. Text appears sooner when you stop speaking or tap stop, and the first words show sooner while you speak. On phones with 6 GB of memory or more, the speech engine stays loaded for five minutes after you use it, so the voice keyboard starts listening at once. The app now shares its colours, dark mode and animations with Marmalade TTS, and the selected tab's name is bold.
