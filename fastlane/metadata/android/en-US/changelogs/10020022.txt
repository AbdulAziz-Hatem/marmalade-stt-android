Marmalade STT 1.2.2 fixes slow results in apps that record their own audio and pass it to Android's speech recognizer, such as Waze's voice commands. Their text now arrives when you stop speaking, as set under End of speech, instead of when the app stops recording, which in Waze was 4–6 seconds later. On slower phones the engine can still lag behind. The audio format these apps send is now read correctly.
