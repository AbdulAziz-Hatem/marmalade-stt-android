Marmalade STT 1.2.3 makes Waze's voice commands finish soon after you stop talking, not 10 seconds in. Voice commands from apps that record their own audio, and searches, now use Auto-stop after a pause instead of Auto-stop with Smart Turn, until you choose an End of speech for other apps in Settings. When the engine can't keep up with an app's audio, the words shown while you speak are skipped to catch up.
