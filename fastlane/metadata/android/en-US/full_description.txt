<p>Marmalade STT turns your speech into text on your phone. Use it as the voice input of your keyboard (FUTO Keyboard, HeliBoard, FlorisBoard and others), and of apps that ask Android for speech input. Your voice never leaves the device.</p>

<ul>
<li><b>Private.</b> Speech is recognized on the phone. Audio is never saved or uploaded</li>
<li><b>Offline.</b> After a one-time download of a speech engine, it works without a connection</li>
<li><b>Live text.</b> Shows your words in real time as you speak, on most engines</li>
<li><b>Knows when you're done.</b> Smart Turn, the default for the speak-now popup, waits until you sound finished, so a pause to think rarely cuts you off</li>
<li><b>Multilingual.</b> 100 languages, with the best engine for yours picked automatically</li>
<li><b>Clean output.</b> Filler words removed, numbers as digits, your own replacements and verbal snippets</li>
<li><b>Free and open source.</b> No ads, no accounts, no analytics</li>
</ul>

<p><b>PRIVATE BY DESIGN</b></p>
<p>No analytics, no tracking, no crash reporting, no accounts, and no server of ours. The internet is used only to download the speech engines you choose, from GitHub, and every file is checked against a pinned SHA-256 hash. Android backup is turned off, so nothing is copied to the cloud.</p>

<p><b>YOU DECIDE WHEN IT STOPS</b></p>
<p>In the speak-now popup and other apps, Marmalade uses Smart Turn v3 technology by default to detect when you have finished speaking. No hands required. Does it cut you off too early? Switch to stopping at a pause, or set an end word instead. On the keyboard it listens until you tap, or it can use Smart Turn too.</p>

<p><b>CLEAN TEXT</b></p>
<p>Optional cleanup fixes capitals, and in English removes filler words, turns "new line" and "period" into the real thing and writes numbers as digits. Replacements for names the engine gets wrong, and snippets for text you type often, work in any language.</p>

<p><b>WORKS WITH YOUR KEYBOARD</b></p>
<p>Turn on "Marmalade voice typing" and your keyboard's mic key opens a small Marmalade panel that types what you say. It works with FUTO Keyboard, HeliBoard, FlorisBoard, AnySoftKeyboard, Unexpected Keyboard, AOSP's keyboard and others with a voice key.</p>

<p><b>AN ENGINE THAT SUITS YOUR PHONE</b></p>
<p>The setup guide recommends a speech engine for your language and for your phone's memory and speed, with the option to download and try out other models.</p>

<p><b>LANGUAGES</b></p>
<p>Fast engines cover English, Bulgarian, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, Estonian, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hungarian, Italian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Maltese, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Slovenian, Spanish, Swedish, Ukrainian, Japanese, Hindi, Arabic and Vietnamese. Chinese and Korean get Qwen3-ASR, lighter than Whisper. You can also pick it for its 28 other languages, English included. Other languages work through Whisper Large v3 Turbo, with more delay.</p>

<p><b>OPEN SOURCE</b></p>
<p>Marmalade STT is MIT licensed. If you'd like to support it, Settings → Support links to GitHub Sponsors; the app is the same either way.</p>
